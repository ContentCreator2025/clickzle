What is Complete the Image?
The Game
Six images. Twenty tiles. One answer.
Complete the Image is a daily perception puzzle. Six images are sliced into 20 tiles and mixed together. Only one image is perfectly intact — the other five have corrupted tiles that can never complete the picture.

You have five minutes. The fastest accurate solve wins the leaderboard.

Every player worldwide plays the exact same puzzle on the same day. One attempt. No retries. A wrong tile sound alerts you the moment you land on a corrupted face — 8 categories available daily.

How to Play
30 seconds to learn
  1. Choose a category
    Pick a category — Movies, Flags, Animals, Art and more. Each has its own independent daily puzzle and live leaderboard.
  2. A 5-second countdown launches the game
    When you enter the game a 5-4-3-2-1 countdown begins automatically. The moment GO fires, the clock is running.
  3. Click tiles to cycle through faces
    Each tile holds six faces — one slice from each of the six images. Click to cycle through them until all 20 tiles show the same image.
  4. Watch for the glitch flash
    Five of the six images are corrupted. When you land on a corrupted face, the tile flickers with a glitch flash. Use this to eliminate decoys.
  5. Snap all 20 tiles into place
    Victory fires the instant all 20 tiles simultaneously show the same complete image. Fastest time wins the board.
