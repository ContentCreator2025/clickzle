What is Hit the Target?
The Game
20 tiles. Four decoy types. Pure visual discrimination.
Hit the Target presents a 4×5 grid of pink tiles. Each tile looks nearly identical. The only difference is a tiny symbol in the centre — a solid dot means shoot it, everything else means leave it alone.

The catch: at speed, a bullseye, a hollow ring, a double ring, and a tiny dim dot all look the same as a target. The game tests your ability to read those differences under pressure, not just how fast you can tap.

Five rounds, one minute each. Score carries through all five. One attempt per day — same daily seed worldwide.

How to Play
5 rounds to master
  1. Read the symbol, not the colour
    Every active tile is pink. The only difference is the tiny symbol in the centre. Solid dot = shoot it. Hollow ring, bullseye, double ring, or tiny dot = leave it. The game lives in that distinction.
  2. Build your streak, build your combo
    Every consecutive clean hit adds to your streak. At 5 hits you reach ×2 combo, doubling every point you score. Maximum is ×5 at a 20-hit streak. One wrong tap resets it to zero.
  3. Tap the gold star for bonus points
    Gold bonus tiles appear from Round 2 and are worth 3× your current combo. They expire faster than regular tiles — the window is short. Missing a bonus never penalises your streak.
  4. Each round introduces harder decoys
    Round 1 uses only rings. Round 2 adds the bullseye — a dot with a ring around it that looks exactly like a target. Round 3 adds double rings and a tiny dim dot. By Round 5 all four decoy types are in play simultaneously.
  5. Sustain your streak to unlock a faster game
    At streak 5+ a new tile spawns the moment you hit one. At streak 20 the game runs almost entirely at your own reaction speed. Average players see ~50 tiles per round. Elite players push past 80. Same 60 seconds — skill determines how far you go.
  6. Wrong taps cost you points — not just your streak
    Hitting a decoy tile breaks your streak and deducts points on the spot: -15 pts for the first error, -20 pts for the second consecutive, -30 pts for the third and beyond. A red deduction pops up instantly on the tile so you always know the cost.
  7. Gold Rush — the elite bonus mode
    In Round 4 or 5 only, build a 50-hit streak with zero wrong taps in that round. If you manage it, GOLD RUSH activates — the grid floods with gold bonus tiles only for 8 seconds. Every tile scores 3× your current combo. It fires once per round and is exceptionally rare — only the very best players will ever trigger it.
