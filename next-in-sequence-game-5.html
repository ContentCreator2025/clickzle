What is Next in Sequence?
The Game
One grid. Rotate the pipes. Connect the flow.
Next in Sequence is a daily reasoning challenge played on a 4×5 grid of 20 pipe tiles. Each round presents a scrambled grid with a START tile and an END tile. Your job: tap tiles to rotate them 90° and build a connected pipe path between the two.

Solve it — and a fresh, harder puzzle appears immediately. The 60-second timer never stops. Every puzzle in a round is harder than the last, with longer paths, more scramble and tighter move budgets.

Some tiles are locked (amber 🔒) — already in position, forming free hints you can route through. Stay within your move budget for a GOLD score. Hit the theoretical minimum for a PERFECT and a significant bonus.

Five rounds, one minute each. Same daily seed for every player worldwide. One attempt.

How to Play
30 seconds to learn
  1. Find START and END
    Every puzzle has a START tile (purple glow) and an END tile (green glow). Your goal is to build a continuous pipe path connecting the two.
  2. Tap tiles to rotate them 90°
    Each tap rotates a tile clockwise. Connected tiles glow purple as the signal flows. The path is complete when the glow reaches END.
  3. Use locked tiles as hints
    Amber 🔒 tiles are already in their correct rotation — they cannot be turned. The solution path runs through them. Use them to anchor your routing.
  4. Stay within your move budget
    Each puzzle has a move budget shown at the top. GOLD score: solve within budget. PERFECT ★: solve with the minimum possible moves for a large bonus. Going over budget still counts — it just costs score.
  5. Chain as many as you can in 60 seconds
    Solve a puzzle and a harder one appears immediately — the timer never stops. Each successive puzzle has a longer path, tighter budget, and more locked tiles. Five rounds. Score carries through.
