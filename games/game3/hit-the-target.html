HOW TO PLAY

Solid dot — Shoot it

Pink tile with a small filled dot. Tap it. Every hit builds your streak and multiplier.

Hollow ring — Don't shoot

Same pink, but the centre is empty. One wrong tap breaks your streak.

Bullseye — Don't shoot

Dot with a ring around it. Appears from Round 2. Looks like shoot — it isn't.

Gold star — Bonus

Worth 3x your current combo. Appears from Round 2. Missing it costs nothing.

5 rounds, 1 minute each

Each round adds more rings and speeds up slightly. Score carries through all 5 rounds. One shot per day.
