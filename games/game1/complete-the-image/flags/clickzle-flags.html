HOW TO PLAY

Six images, one winner

Six images sliced into 20 tiles and mixed together. Only ONE is complete — the other five have corrupted tiles that make them impossible to finish.

5-second countdown, then GO

Countdown fires the moment you dismiss this. The 5-minute clock starts on GO. You cannot pause.

Click to reveal & cycle

First click reveals a tile. Every click after cycles through all six faces. Get all 20 tiles showing the same image to win.

Glitch flash = corrupted

Tile flickers and buzzes? That face is corrupt and can never be the answer. Use it to eliminate decoys fast.
