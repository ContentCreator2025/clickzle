HOW TO PLAY

Connect Start to End

Tap any tile to rotate it 90°. Connect a pipe path from START to END. When the path is complete the round counts and a fresh puzzle appears — timer keeps running.

Tile types
Normal tile — tap to rotate 90°. Each tap uses 1 move from your budget.
🔒
Locked tile 🔒 — already in the correct position. Can't be rotated. The path runs through it — use it as a hint.
Live tile — purple glow means signal is flowing through this tile. Connect all the way to END to complete the puzzle.

Move budget & scoring

Each puzzle has a move budget shown at the top. Stay within it for GOLD. Hit the theoretical minimum for PERFECT ★ and a big bonus. Going over budget only hurts your score — you can still complete the puzzle.

5 rounds, 1 minute each

60 seconds per round. Puzzles get harder after each solve. Score carries across all five rounds. One attempt per day — same sequence worldwide.
