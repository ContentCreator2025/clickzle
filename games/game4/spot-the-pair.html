HOW TO PLAY

Find the matching pair

20 tiles fill the grid. Exactly one pair of identical icons is hidden among them. All other tiles are unique.

Tap first, then confirm

Tap one tile to select it — it glows yellow. Find its exact match and tap it. Correct: both dissolve, new grid spawns instantly.

Watch out for decoys

Near-identical icons appear later. They look similar but are not the pair. The pair is always identical — not just similar.

5 rounds, 1 minute each

Score carries. One wrong tap resets your streak and briefly stuns the grid. One chance per day.
