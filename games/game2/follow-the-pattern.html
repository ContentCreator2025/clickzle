HOW TO PLAY

WATCH the tiles flash

The grid dims and tiles light up one at a time. Each tile plays a unique musical tone. Watch carefully and memorise the sequence.

YOUR TURN — tap them back

When the badge says YOUR TURN, tap the tiles in the exact order they flashed. Correct tiles glow green.

Sequences grow longer

Each completed sequence adds one more tile. A large SEQUENCE BONUS score pops on screen showing exactly what you earned. A wrong tile ends the sequence and costs you those points.

5 rounds, 1 minute each

A 3-2-1-GO countdown fires before each round. Complete as many sequences as possible in each minute. Score accumulates across all five rounds.
