What is Speed the Difference?
The Game
Same image. Hidden differences.
Speed the Difference splits the interface into two halves — top and bottom. Both show the same image, but the bottom half contains hidden changes. The entire interface is divided into small squares. Tap any square where you think a difference exists.

Correct — it lights up and counts toward your total. Wrong — time penalty. Find every difference before the clock expires to complete the puzzle.

90 seconds. 5 to 7 differences per puzzle. Fresh images every day.

How to Play
30 seconds to learn
  1. Study both halves
    The top half is the original. The bottom half looks identical — but contains hidden differences. Take a moment to scan before you tap.
  2. Tap the square where you see a difference
    The interface is divided into small squares. Tap a square to mark it as different. Correct marks light up in your accent colour.
  3. Wrong taps cost time
    An incorrect tap adds a time penalty. Think before you mark — accuracy matters as much as speed.
  4. Find all differences to complete the puzzle
    Once you've found every difference the puzzle is complete. Your time is locked in and your score is calculated.
  5. Race the clock
    You have 90 seconds. Finding all differences early means a higher score. Every second counts.
